Project Overview

Crimson Moon is a gothic action-adventure RPG currently in development at C2 Game Studio, co-developed with ProbablyMonsters, the independent AAA studio behind titles like Everspace 2. The game is set in Gildenarch, a cursed city overrun by demons, undead, and the Infernal Legion's return, where players take on the role of a Nephilim: a violent half-human, half-angel hybrid fighting to reclaim what was lost.

The game features souls-inspired melee combat, replayable runs, scalable challenges, and two-player co-op, all framed within a dense, handcrafted gothic world. Each district of Gildenarch has its own identity, its own darkness, and its own secrets to uncover before the final confrontation with the vampire lord Solomon Moore.

Crimson Moon is coming in 2026 to PS5, Xbox Series X|S, PC, and Epic Games Store.

My Contribution

I am C2 Game Studio's Lead Level Designer on Crimson Moon, working directly alongside ProbablyMonsters' lead designer. My day-to-day work involves spatial problem-solving, area design contributions, and close cross-discipline collaboration with the combat, narrative, and systems design teams across both studios.

Souls-like level design is a genre I've studied deeply as a player: it's the design vocabulary behind what drew me to level design in the first place. Working on Crimson Moon means applying that knowledge professionally, in a genre where spatial design isn't just decoration but the central medium through which challenge, discovery, and atmosphere are communicated to the player.

Due to the active NDA on this project, I'm not able to share work-in-progress content, documentation, or level-specific details.
